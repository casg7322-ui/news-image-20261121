1Iran strikes Bahrain and tankers in the GulfCLAIMREPORTED

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards say missiles and drones hit two US Patriot radars at bases in Bahrain on Saturday night; Bahrain says only a civilian industrial site burned, with no casualties. Hours earlier, two tankers were hit off Oman’s Khasab and a third struck a mine east of Hormuz.

Reuters / UKMTO / IRGC via Sepah News · 4th attack on Bahrain in 9 days

2Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel bossREPORTED

Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, “El Chapo Isidro”, in Guasave after a gunfight that left 11 dead; he was flown to Mexico City on Saturday as cartel gunmen burned vehicles and blocked roads.

3Climate summit in overtime as fossil-fuel fight drags onREPORTED
